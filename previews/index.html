Direction D — you liked B, this is further

The reading

The page performs an ATS parse. Drag the beam: left of it is the text a machine extracted, right of it is the design you made. Two layers, one custom property, pixel-locked at any drag speed. Losses surface as the beam passes them.

Versatile by tone: dark instrument sections and paper sections alternate, and the mast inverts with the paper. Tiltable drag specimen, a nine-card template plate, a peel that uncovers the parse, a 40-tick gauge, magnetic buttons, a custom cursor. Six mechanisms, all on one page.

How these were built

A, B and C came from three agents on one brief each. D and E were built directly, with a higher bar: each is organised around a single hard interaction rather than a motion language — a drag-tracked dual clip-path lens in D, a spring-damper with real angular momentum in E. Shared rules across all five: motion on transform/opacity/clip-path/stroke only, full prefers-reduced-motion resolution, no horizontal overflow at 320–1920, no decorative gradients, and a ban on the usual tells (centred hero with a blob, three icon cards, "Seamlessly", glassmorphism). All five pass node previews/audit.mjs — 10 checks each.
