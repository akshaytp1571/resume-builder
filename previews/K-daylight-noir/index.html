How it works

From paste to polished.

Step 01

Paste the posting.

Drop in any job description. We read the whole thing — the requirements, the responsibilities, and the exact language they use.

Step 02

We parse your résumé.

Every section, date, and bullet becomes structured data a machine can actually read — no guessing where your experience ends.

Step 03

Then we score it.

The score reads you the way a machine does. We extract each section, match its language against the posting, and weight four things: parse fidelity, keyword coverage, formatting risk, and seniority match. A 91 means you're legible — with a few gaps worth closing.

Step 04

Tailor and export.

Rewrite the weak bullets, keep your voice, and export clean plain text or a typeset PDF that reads well to machines and people alike.

How the score works

Four signals, one number.

An ATS score is a 0–100 estimate of how cleanly an applicant tracking system can read your résumé as structured data. Here's exactly what goes into it.

parse_fidelity 96

How cleanly a machine can split your résumé into sections, dates, and bullets — the foundation everything else is measured against.

keyword_coverage 90

How many of the posting's required terms appear in your own words — matched by meaning, not just exact strings.

formatting_risk 84

How much of your layout survives a plain-text pass. Columns, tables, and text boxes are the usual casualties.

seniority_match 93

How closely your titles, scope, and years of experience line up with the level they're actually hiring for.

No black box — every point traces to a line in your résumé.

The optional extras

Power-user tooling, not magic.

Optional

Bring your own AI key

Connect an AI provider to rewrite bullets in your voice and suggest tighter phrasing. Your key stays in your browser and nothing is proxied through us. Switch it off and the studio scores, parses, and exports exactly the same.

Off by default. The core tool never needs it.

Optional

Local job agent

A small Playwright agent runs on your own machine, opens a live posting, reads it, and drafts a résumé tailored to the real text — no copy-paste, no cloud round-trip. You review every change before it lands.

Runs locally. Nothing is uploaded to tailor.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it upload my résumé?+
No. Parsing, scoring, and export all run in this browser. Your résumé is written to localStorage and never sent anywhere — unless you explicitly connect an AI provider with your own key.
What is an ATS score?+
A 0–100 estimate of how cleanly an applicant tracking system can read your résumé as structured data. It weighs parse fidelity, keyword coverage, formatting risk, and seniority match — and every point traces back to a specific line.
Can I use my own AI key?+
Yes. Bring a key from a supported provider and it's stored only in your browser. The tool works fully without it — AI rewriting is strictly optional and off by default.
Is it really free?+
Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, runs entirely on your machine, and has no accounts, no paywalls, and no telemetry.
