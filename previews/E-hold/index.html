What it is

A résumé has two sides.
You only write one.

  1. 01

    The front is yours

    Nine templates, real A4 and US Letter, export to vector PDF, DOCX and JSON.

  2. 02

    The back is theirs

    The parser view flattens your page into the single column a tracking tool extracts, and shows every field it reads.

  3. 03

    Scored in your tab

    The ATS score is computed locally from your actual rendered page — weightings you can inspect.

  4. 04

    Nothing leaves

    No account, no server, no upload. Backup to your own Google Drive if you want to.

Turn it over yourself.
Then go fix the back.
