03

03 Function

Six things, all of them real.

  1. 01

    ATS scoring

    Layout-aware. It reads structure — section order, headings, columns — not just a keyword list. It names the line that costs you points.

    Live
  2. 02

    Keyword match

    Paste the posting. Get the terms you are missing, and how often the filter expects them. No guessing at synonyms.

    Per job
  3. 03

    Parser view

    The document as flattened text. See the fields that vanish before a human ever opens the file.

    Built in
  4. 04

    Cover letters

    One per variant, drafted from your own summary and most recent role. Exports as a business letter, not a résumé with a paragraph on top.

    Paired
  5. 05

    Application tracker

    Every application, its stage and its date. Stored in the browser, beside the résumés it refers to.

    Local
  6. 06

    Apply agent

    An optional Playwright agent that fills the form from your profile. You read it, you correct it, you submit. It never presses the last button.

    Optional
