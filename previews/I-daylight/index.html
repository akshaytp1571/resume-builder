How it works

From paste to polished.

Step 01

Paste the posting.

Drop in any job description. We read the whole thing — the requirements, the responsibilities, and the exact language they use.

Step 02

We parse your résumé.

Every section, date, and bullet becomes structured data a machine can actually read — no guessing where your experience ends.

Step 03

Then we score it.

The score reads you the way a machine does. We extract each section, match its language against the posting, and weight three things: keyword coverage, section completeness, and whether your formatting survives a plain-text pass. A 91 means you're legible — with a few gaps worth closing.

Step 04

Tailor and export.

Rewrite the weak bullets, keep your voice, and export clean plain text or a typeset PDF that reads well to machines and people alike.

The optional extras

Power-user tooling, not magic.

Optional

Bring your own AI key

Connect an AI provider to rewrite bullets in your voice and suggest tighter phrasing. Your key stays in your browser and nothing is proxied through us. Switch it off and the studio scores, parses, and exports exactly the same.

Off by default. The core tool never needs it.

Optional

Local job agent

A small Playwright agent runs on your own machine, opens a live posting, reads it, and drafts a résumé tailored to the real text — no copy-paste, no cloud round-trip. You review every change before it lands.

Runs locally. Nothing is uploaded to tailor.
